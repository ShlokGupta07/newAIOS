Why it matters

Lives lost when emergency care isn't there in time

India road crash deaths and the case for faster response.

168,491 / 172,890road deaths in 2022 / 2023 (MoRTH)
~50%of road crash deaths can be averted with definitive care within the first hour
25–35 minambulance arrival on core highways; only ~75% of ambulances reach the scene
90% / 95%of road ambulances lacked essential equipment / had untrained staff

Potentially avoidable deaths (~50%)

202284,246 of 168,491
202386,445 of 172,890

Avoidable split is an estimate: the ~50% figure applied to reported totals. Sources: MoRTH Road Accidents in India 2022 and 2023; Scroll.in (Death by delay); Indian highway ambulance survey (J Family Med Prim Care).
